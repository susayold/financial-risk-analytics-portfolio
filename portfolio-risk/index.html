08 · Portfolio takeaways

What the portfolio evidence says

01

Observed risk is unevenly distributed

Risk varies meaningfully across segments and dimensions.

02

Riskiest is not always most material

BAD rate and BAD-associated amount answer different questions.

03

Concentration matters

A small number of segments drive a large share of observed risk.

04

Time changes the portfolio

Observed cohorts vary across time and need truncation-aware reading.
