The plan does not define a native numeric percentage scale. The score below is

The machine-readable companion is generated by
`src/build_block_d_completion_scorecard.py`; it is checked by the full-review
QA so the displayed stage count and percentages cannot silently drift.

## Scoring method

Two measures are shown separately:

1. **Execution coverage** — whether the planned analytical work and review
   evidence were executed and recorded. All D0–D9 stages are currently 100% on
   this measure.
2. **Closure readiness** — how close the stage is to satisfying the final
   approved/locked gate, including limitations and pending owner decisions.

Closure-readiness mapping used for this scorecard:

| Recorded gate state | Conversion |
|---|---:|
| `PASS` | 100% |
| `PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS` | 90% |
| `BRIDGE_RECONCILED_APPROVAL_PENDING` | 75% |
| `CONTROLLED_HOLD` with executed review pack | 60% |
| `NOT_LOCKED_REVIEW_REQUIRED` at final closure | 50% |

The Block D closure-readiness score is the simple average of the ten stage
scores: `(100 + 90 + 90 + 90 + 75 + 60 + 60 + 60 + 60 + 50) / 10 = 73.5%`.
