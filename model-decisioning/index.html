10 · Model takeaways

What the model evidence says

01

Strong historical OOT discrimination

The frozen C8E model reaches 0.8558 ROC-AUC and 0.5625 KS on the one-time 2017 OOT cohort.

02

Ranking is cleaner than calibration

Risk deciles remain monotonic, while calibration slope sits just outside the GREEN boundary and remains under monitoring.

03

Leakage discipline matters more than headline AUC

Models above 0.95 AUC were rejected when they relied on information unavailable at origination.

04

The score is a governed input

p_bad_final becomes a frozen percentile, decile and risk band before downstream loss and policy analytics.
