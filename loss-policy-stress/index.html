P04-13 · EXECUTIVE TAKEAWAYS

The model becomes decision economics

01

Risk ranking becomes portfolio economics

The frozen score is converted into analytical severity, exposure and expected-loss views without reopening the model.

02

Policy is a trade-off, not one optimal cutoff

Growth, Balanced and Conservative scenarios expose approval, BAD capture and GOOD route-out trade-offs.

03

Pricing evidence is intentionally bounded

Observed rates are compared with analytical EL, but profitability is not claimed without governed cost and capital inputs.

04

Stress is transparent and reversible

Scenario loss rates show explicit drivers and reverse-stress breakpoints rather than opaque forecast labels.
